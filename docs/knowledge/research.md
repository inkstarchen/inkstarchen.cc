### 科学研究的本质
Research is the pursuit of new knowledge through the process of discovery. Scientific research involves diligent inquiry and systematic observation of phenomena. Most scientific research projects incolve experimentation, often requiring testing the effect of changing conditions on the results. The conditions under which specific observations are made must be carefully controlled and records must be meticulously maintained. This ensures that observations and results can be are reproduced. Scienctific research can be basic (fundamental) or applied.

### How to be a good researcher
- Observe 
	- be patient, take time to look over and over again
- Keep asking questions
	- why and how, question your results, others' results
- What do you expect? Do things add up?
	- does everything agree or are you finding discrepancies? is everything as you expect it to be? If you change your view point, do things make sense? Are there cases your results do not hold?
- What is the biggest picture?
	- take a step back? Can you heneralize your results? Can you consider potential conditions where your results do not hold?
- Persevere
	- Science is 95% hard work, often painstaking work, mundane work, sometimes it fails or produces uninteresting results, etc Don't be disappointed, persist. Keep moving.
- Collaborate 
	- No scientist works alone. Every genius stands on the shoulders of other geniuses. You needs others to bounce ideas, to get help, assistance and support. Always work with others. Today more than ever.
- Communication
	- Always publicize your work, through meeting, workshops, talks and media announcements.
